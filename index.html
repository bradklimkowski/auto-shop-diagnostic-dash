Auto Shop Diagnostic Dash Student Instructions

Goal

Answer as many automotive questions as you can before time runs out. The goal is to practice real Autos 2 content and improve your diagnostic thinking.

How to Play

  1. Open the game link on your Chromebook.
  2. Choose a category or select Mixed Review.
  3. Click Start Game.
  4. Read each question carefully.
  5. Select the best answer before time runs out.
  6. After each question, read the explanation.
  7. Keep going until the timer ends.
  8. Review your score, accuracy, and number of questions attempted.

Scoring

  • Correct answer: 100 points
  • Fast correct answers may earn a speed bonus.
  • Wrong answers earn 0 points.
  • Streak bonuses may be awarded for several correct answers in a row.

Classroom Expectations

  • Think like a technician.
  • Read the full question before answering.
  • Choose the best diagnostic step, not just the fastest repair.
  • Do not randomly click answers.
  • Do not search the internet during the game.
  • Do not distract other students.
  • Use missed questions as review.

Diagnostic Mindset

The best answer is often the best first test or inspection, not replacing a part. Verify the concern, check the basics, test before replacing parts, use evidence, and make the repair decision last.

After the Round

Review your final score, number correct, number attempted, and accuracy percentage. Be ready to identify one topic you need to review.
